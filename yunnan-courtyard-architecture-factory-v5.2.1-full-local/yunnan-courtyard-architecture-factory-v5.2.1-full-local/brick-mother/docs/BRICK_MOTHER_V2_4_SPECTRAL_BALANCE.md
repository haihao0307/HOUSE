# Brick Mother V2.4 Spectral Balance and Material Scale

V2.4 responds to the V2.3 browser evidence.

## Fired clay

Broad kiln-hot, kiln-ash, neutral, oxide, soot, mineral, wet, and biological zones now carry distinct hues. High-frequency noise no longer dominates the color identity.

## Stone

Stone midtones are lifted while cavity and AO darkening are reduced. RockMap and strata remain structural drivers, with lower micro-frequency color and bump influence.

## Adobe

Straw, husk, seed, and pull-out pits use a shared low-frequency burial field. This forms dense and sparse inclusion regions instead of a uniform scatter.

## Approval

Browser and source gates validate operation only. JRB color, stone detail, adobe inclusions, and production approval remain manual.
