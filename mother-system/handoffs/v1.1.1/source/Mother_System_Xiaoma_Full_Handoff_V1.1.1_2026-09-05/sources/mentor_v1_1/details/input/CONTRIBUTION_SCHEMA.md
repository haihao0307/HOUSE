# Contribution Schema for Open Knowledge

A future contribution should contain:

```yaml
contribution_id:
contributor:
contributor_role:
region:
place:
time_range:
object_type:
claim:
evidence_type:
source_reference:
source_date:
capture_date:
coordinates:
measurement_units:
confidence:
contradictions:
review_status:
reviewer:
license_or_usage_rights:
notes:
```

## Evidence types

- direct measurement
- archival document
- historical photograph
- modern photograph
- oral history
- expert testimony
- academic publication
- field observation
- map
- satellite
- SAR
- aerial photo
- scan
- physical artifact
- museum record
- inferred reconstruction

## Review states

- submitted
- source_verified
- domain_reviewed
- conflicting
- accepted
- rejected
- superseded

## Rule

A contribution may be valuable even when uncertain.

Uncertainty should be preserved rather than erased.
