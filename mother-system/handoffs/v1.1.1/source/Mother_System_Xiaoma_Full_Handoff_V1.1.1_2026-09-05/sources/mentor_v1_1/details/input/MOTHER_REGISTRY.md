# Mother Registry

This registry is conceptual. Individual repository names may change.

## A. World / Terrain / Environment family

### Landscape Mother
Scope:
- landform generation
- karst
- mountain systems
- islands
- valleys
- erosion morphology
- close-range terrain quality
- procedural terrain
- DEM relation

Known examples:
- Guilin
- Kunming
- Yulong Snow Mountain style environments
- karst systems
- island landscapes

### Weather Mother
Scope:
- atmosphere
- rain
- snow
- fog
- clouds
- lightning
- wind
- lighting interactions
- weather response of world materials

### Ocean Mother
Scope:
- deep sea
- coastal water
- coast
- shore interaction
- waves
- foam
- spray
- buoyancy
- collision
- near-shore dynamics

### Future environment Mothers
- Rain
- Snow
- Fog
- Cloud
- River
- Plant
- Soil
- Ecology
